The engagement

I served as team leader on a consulting engagement for GoBuild360, a South Africa-based construction technology company. The team analyzed U.S. market entry for the company, applying the same structured approach I use on every client problem: define the question clearly, gather evidence, and deliver a recommendation the client can act on.

What we delivered

The team delivered a market analysis report and recommendations for breaking into adjacent industries and countries.

What I took away

Working on a problem set in a market far from home reinforced how much context matters. Assumptions that hold in one country can fail in another, which makes local knowledge, careful research, and humility about what you do not yet know central to good advice.
