Green Tree Risk Group: Start-Up Groundwork

Building the foundation for an insurtech company focused on AI liability and cyber insurance.

Role

Venture
Insurtech start-up
Focus
AI liability and cyber insurance

Groundwork

The idea

Green Tree Risk Group is built around two engines. The first is a managing general agent (MGA) platform, a specialized underwriting partner that designs and manages insurance programs on behalf of carriers, focused on AI liability and cyber insurance. The second is software that helps organizations govern how they use AI. The thesis is that organizations adopting AI need both better coverage and better controls, and that each side strengthens the other.

Customer discovery

As COO and co-founder, I helped lead the early groundwork. We worked through the Business Model Canvas block by block, grounding each one in more than ten customer discovery interviews. I coordinated weekly interview rounds across a team of six and turned what we heard into decisions about revenue streams, customer relationships, and channels.

Financial model and valuation

I built the company's six-year financial model from scratch, including partnership structures, a detailed expense build, and a balanced balance sheet, with cell-level source notes so every assumption could be traced. I also developed a valuation approach based on public market multiples that values the underwriting and software businesses separately and then combines them.

Positioning and investor materials

Alongside the numbers, I researched market sizing and pricing benchmarks, led work on the company's name and brand identity, and produced investor-ready materials, including a pitch deck and brand guidelines.

What I took away

Start-up groundwork is mostly disciplined learning: testing assumptions with real customers before committing money to them, and building models that show their work. It is the kind of work my entrepreneurship concentration prepared me for, and it has shaped how I approach every new venture since.
