MBA Candidate | Veteran | Dad

I’m an MBA candidate at the University of Oklahoma and a U.S. Army veteran. I served for 10 years as an engineer officer, leading soldiers and managing construction, facilities, and logistics programs, and I later worked as a capital planning consultant assessing large facility portfolios for universities, healthcare systems, and government clients. I also hold a PMP certification and a master’s degree in geological engineering. The common thread is a focus on discipline, clarity, and building systems that people can actually use.

I’m preparing for a career in strategy and operations, with particular interest in aerospace, defense, and industries that matter to Oklahoma and beyond. My MBA work has given me hands-on consulting experience across real estate management, healthcare technology, and international market entry, and I’ve helped build an insurtech start-up from the ground up. I also founded BoomTown Home Services, a veteran-owned company serving homeowners in the Oklahoma City area, which keeps me close to the realities of running a business.

Outside of work, I’m a husband and father of two, which is the most important leadership role I’ll ever have. I read widely, from history and biographies to fast-paced thrillers, and I enjoy conversations that challenge me to think differently. This site is one way I share the bigger picture of who I am: not just a resume or a career path, but the full mix of experiences, interests, and commitments that shape how I approach life and work.
