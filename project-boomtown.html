Why I started it

I founded BoomTown Home Services in July 2024, shortly after leaving the Army, with a simple goal: help homeowners in the Oklahoma City area care for the places they love, season after season, year after year. It is veteran-owned and built around habits the Army taught me, such as showing up on time, communicating clearly, and finishing what we start.

Running the business

Because it is a small company, I work across every part of it: the services we offer, how jobs are scheduled and delivered, how customers hear from us, invoicing, and marketing. At that scale, every process decision shows up in the customer's experience, which has been a useful education for an operations-minded MBA student.

The work covers all things residential home maintenance and preventive care: routine visits, seasonal upkeep, and one-off jobs. Over the course of a year the business serves roughly 50 or more households. I run scheduling and day-to-day coordination with Google's free tools, which keeps overhead low and the process easy to repeat.

What it has taught me

Running BoomTown made my entrepreneurship coursework concrete: pricing against real costs, managing cash flow in a seasonal business, and building repeatable processes so quality does not depend on any one person. It also keeps me close to the daily realities of the customers and operators I hope to advise as a consultant.
