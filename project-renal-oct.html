Renal OCT Consulting Engagement

A five-person consulting team developing a commercialization strategy for a renal optical coherence tomography technology.

Client
OCTENDOTA, LLC
Program partner
OKBioStart
Team
Five consultants
Focus
Commercialization strategy

The engagement

Our team of five consulted for OCTENDOTA, LLC, in partnership with OKBioStart, on how to bring a renal optical coherence tomography (OCT) technology toward commercialization. Early-stage medical technology raises several questions at once: who would use it and why, how large and reachable the market is, how it compares with current alternatives, and what it will take to get from a promising device to a viable business.

My role

As one of five team members, I helped organize those questions into a structured recommendation and kept the work tied to the decisions the client actually had to make.

What we delivered

The engagement produced a set of recommendations for taking the technology toward market, supported by research and analysis the client could build on.

What I took away

Healthcare technology taught me how much a good commercialization plan depends on translating technical strengths into the language of buyers, regulators, and investors. It was also a useful lesson in dividing work across a small team and bringing the pieces back together into one clear story.
